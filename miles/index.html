Miles per migrant

How far a person is moved through the detention system before the case ends -- path length (every facility-to-facility leg summed), net displacement (first facility to last, straight-line), and tortuosity (the ratio of the two). Every figure below is an aggregate; no individual case is shown or traceable from this page.

616,764 stays, the atlas's own study window (2025-01-20 onward). Full method and data-quality audit: the book's "How far people are moved through the system" section and notes/miles-per-migrant-profile.md.

Distribution of distance moved (path length, floor)

Straight-line (WGS84 geodesic) floor, per stay -- log-scale bins, since the distribution is heavily right-skewed (median 290.9 mi, a small share of stays above 5,000 mi). Never read the floor figure as "distance travelled" -- real road distance typically runs 1.2-1.4x this.
