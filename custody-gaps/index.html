Unrecorded custody gaps (minors)

A detained minor's own stay can show a gap this project's underlying detention data never recorded -- days between a recorded stint and a stay's own end, with nothing in between. ICE is separately required to report its own custody of minors, including hotel and transport stays, to a federal court (Flores v. Reno, C.D. Cal. No. 2:85-cv-04544) -- a real source this project now matches against its own records for a confirmed subset. Where no court record exists at all, a stay showing the same pattern is flagged as an inference, never asserted to be a hotel or any specific kind of custody.

Every figure below is an aggregate (a count, a sum) -- no individual case is named or traceable from this page. Any facility/month cell or daily count under 5 is omitted entirely before this page ever sees it, not rounded or shown as "<5". Full methodology, numbers, and open questions: the book's Interface chapter.
