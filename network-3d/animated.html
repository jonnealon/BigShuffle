Prototype, 2026-09-22: height = each facility's own real net flow direction over the whole period (outgoing minus incoming, as a share of its total) -- net exporters (intake) at the base, net importers (exit-heavy) toward the apex, balanced facilities mid-height. Off = original free-floating layout.
Edges are directed, real recorded flow (source -> destination) -- not one person's whole path. Entry/intake facilities read as sources; terminal/ exit-dominant facilities read as sinks, because that's what the real direction shows, not a special case for this page. Node/edge colors are the same 6 that fail this project's own accessibility check (contrast/color-vision gates) -- shown for consistency with the map, not because they passed that check. Entries/exits only count at a facility that already has a transfer edge of its own -- a facility where someone was booked in and released/removed without ever being moved elsewhere has no node here to attach that count to (96.2%/99.1% coverage; see data/build_network_3d_directed.py). Exit doesn't yet distinguish removal from release/bond/other -- it's every recorded departure, one color.
